### Some of the more interesting or unusual graphs

These would make your project stand out:

1. **"Years in NATO vs Interoperability Score"**

   * X: `Years_In_NATO`
   * Y: `Interoperability_Score`
   * Question: Does being in NATO longer make a country more integrated?

2. **"Training Exercises vs Defense Spending"**

   * X: `Training_Exercises_Per_Year`
   * Y: `Defense_Budget_Billion_USD`
   * Question: Are countries spending money on actual readiness?

3. **"Military Personnel per Million Citizens"**

   * Compute: `Total_Military_Personnel / Population_M`
   * Compare countries normalized by population.

4. **Animated Scatter Plot**

   * X: `GDP_Billion_USD`
   * Y: `Defense_Budget_Billion_USD`
   * Bubble size: `Total_Military_Personnel`
   * Animation frame: `Year`
   * This would show how NATO countries evolve economically and militarily over time.

5. **Heatmap of Countries vs Years**

   * Rows: `Country`
   * Columns: `Year`
   * Color: `Defense_GDP_Percent`
   * You could immediately see which countries increased military spending after events like the Russian invasion of Ukraine.

For a NATO dashboard, I think the strongest combination would be:

* World map of NATO expansion (you already have this)
* Defense spending by country
* Interoperability score ranking
* Training exercises vs interoperability scatter plot
* Heatmap of defense spending over time
* Bubble chart of GDP vs military size vs defense budget

Those together would tell a story of NATO's growth, economic power, military power, and operational readiness.
